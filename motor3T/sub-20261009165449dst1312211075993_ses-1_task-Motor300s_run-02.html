Motor300s run-02 task analysis

← subject report. Prototype first-level GLM (glm.py).

Methods

Single-echo run. Distortion correction: phasediff fieldmap (magnitude1 mask) + SynthSR residual warp. Motion correction, distortion correction and the BOLD→T1w registration were applied in one interpolation.

The preprocessed series in T1w space (150 volumes, TR 2 s) was smoothed with a 5 mm FWHM Gaussian. 0 non-steady-state volumes were dropped from the model. Design matrix: left and right block regressors (onsets and durations from the events file) convolved with a double-gamma HRF (SPM canonical: gamma shapes 6 and 16 s, undershoot ratio 1:6) and sampled at each volume's StartTime (slice-timing or FRISGO-spline reference; mid-TR if absent); an intercept; a discrete cosine basis with a 128 s high-pass cutoff; and the 6 rigid-body motion parameters (3 translations, 3 rotations) from head-motion estimation, without derivatives or squares. Other confounds (global, white-matter and CSF signals, CompCor, framewise displacement, DVARS) are in the confounds file but were not used. Serial correlation: lag-1 autocorrelation of the OLS residuals, spatially smoothed (5 mm), then a Prais-Winsten AR(1) refit per voxel. Contrasts: [1 −1], [−1 1], [1 0], [0 1]; df 137, stored in each t map's NIfTI intent. Figures show t ≥ 3.15 (p < 0.001, one-sided, uncorrected for multiple comparisons).
