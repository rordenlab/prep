Methods

Imaging procedure

Imaging data were acquired on a Siemens MAGNETOM Terra.X 7 T scanner (receive coil: 8Tx32Rx_Head_C). A 3D T1-weighted anatomical image (MPRAGE) was acquired with 0.63×0.63×0.63 mm voxels, a 288×384×384 matrix, repetition time (TR) 6000 ms, echo time (TE) 2.06 ms. One fMRI series (task-motor acq-3d1pt0mm run-04) was acquired with a 3D gradient-echo EPI sequence: TR 3.4 s; TE 22 ms; flip angle 13°; 200×200 matrix; 200×200 mm field of view; 120 1-mm partitions; ×2 in-plane acceleration; anterior-to-posterior phase encoding; 87 volumes. One fMRI series (task-motor acq-cmrr2d3echo2mmPFnoFatSat run-03) was acquired with a multiband gradient-echo EPI (3 echoes, magnitude and phase) sequence: TR 1 s; TEs 10.4, 24.5, 38.6 ms; flip angle 35°; 104×104 matrix; 208×208 mm field of view; 63 interleaved 2-mm-thick slices; ×4 in-plane acceleration; ×3 multiband; anterior-to-posterior phase encoding; 316 volumes.

Preprocessing

Data were preprocessed with fastprep, which follows the fMRIPrep workflow [1] using permissively licensed tools, with voxel-wise operations in niimath [2]. The T1-weighted image was cropped to a robust field of view excluding the neck (niimath implementation of FSL's robustfov [3]), corrected for intensity non-uniformity with niimath's implementation of N4 [4], brain-extracted with MindGrab [5], and segmented into grey matter, white matter and cerebrospinal fluid partial-volume maps with the brainchop MindMap model [6]; cortical surfaces were reconstructed with TopoFit [7]. It was normalised to the MNI152NLin2009cAsym template [8] with Greedy [9]: an affine brain-to-brain registration followed by a diffeomorphic (stationary velocity field) registration, both maximising normalised mutual information.

For each fMRI run, non-steady-state volumes were detected and a reference volume was computed as in fMRIPrep, and head motion was estimated by six-parameter rigid-body registration of every volume to that reference; multi-echo runs were first combined by T2*-weighted optimal combination [10]. Runs acquired with alternating (dual) read polarity were first corrected for fuzzy ripple artefacts [11] with niimath's implementation of LayNii's LN2_FRISGO [12], removing each voxel's mean alternation between odd and even volumes, which leaves volume timing unchanged. Susceptibility distortion was handled per run (below). The corrected reference was brain-extracted with MindGrab and registered to the T1-weighted image with niimath's implementation of AFNI's 3dAllineate [13], using the local Pearson correlation cost [14] and a 12-parameter affine transform. Slice-timing correction (Fourier phase shift to the middle of each volume's acquisition) was applied to runs with slice-timing information; that reference time is recorded as StartTime in each output sidecar. Head-motion correction, distortion correction, coregistration and normalisation were composed into a single cubic B-spline interpolation, giving data in T1w space (at the functional resolution) and in MNI152NLin2009cAsym space. Confounds were computed as in fMRIPrep, including framewise displacement and DVARS [15] and anatomical and temporal CompCor components [16]. Temporal SNR was the mean divided by the residual standard deviation after regressing polynomial trends and the six motion parameters. No spatial smoothing or temporal filtering was applied.

Distortion correction for task-motor acq-3d1pt0mm run-04: with no field map, a field-map-less correction was used: the reference was converted to a synthetic T1-weighted image with SynthSR [17], and a diffeomorphic Greedy [9] registration of the subject's brain-extracted T1-weighted image to it (both brain-extracted with MindGrab) gave a 3D displacement field applied after coregistration.

Distortion correction for task-motor acq-cmrr2d3echo2mmPFnoFatSat run-03: frame-wise field maps were estimated from the multi-echo phase with MEDIC [18] (niimath implementation) and applied as per-volume voxel shifts along the phase-encoding direction, followed by a residual correction: the reference was converted to a synthetic T1-weighted image with SynthSR [17], and a diffeomorphic Greedy [9] registration of the subject's brain-extracted T1-weighted image to it (both brain-extracted with MindGrab) gave a 3D displacement field applied after coregistration.
