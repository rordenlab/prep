Methods

Imaging procedure

Imaging data were acquired on a Siemens Prisma_fit 3 T scanner (receive coil: Head_32). A 3D T1-weighted anatomical image was acquired with 1×1×1 mm voxels, a 176×256×256 matrix, repetition time (TR) 2530 ms, echo time (TE) 1.69 ms, inversion time (TI) 1250 ms, flip angle 7°, ×2 in-plane acceleration. One fMRI series (task-rest) was acquired with a multiband gradient-echo EPI sequence: TR 0.8 s; TE 37 ms; flip angle 52°; 104×104 matrix; 208×208 mm field of view; 72 interleaved 2-mm-thick slices; ×8 multiband; anterior-to-posterior phase encoding; 600 volumes.

Preprocessing

Data were preprocessed with fastprep, which follows the fMRIPrep workflow [1] using permissively licensed tools, with voxel-wise operations in niimath [2]. The T1-weighted image was cropped to a robust field of view excluding the neck (niimath implementation of FSL's robustfov [3]), corrected for intensity non-uniformity with niimath's implementation of N4 [4], brain-extracted with MindGrab [5], and segmented into grey matter, white matter and cerebrospinal fluid partial-volume maps with the brainchop MindMap model [6]; cortical surfaces were reconstructed with TopoFit [7]. It was normalised to the MNI152NLin2009cAsym template [8] with Greedy [9]: an affine brain-to-brain registration followed by a diffeomorphic (stationary velocity field) registration, both maximising normalised mutual information.

For each fMRI run, non-steady-state volumes were detected and a reference volume was computed as in fMRIPrep, and head motion was estimated by six-parameter rigid-body registration of every volume to that reference. Susceptibility distortion was handled per run (below). The corrected reference was brain-extracted with MindGrab and registered to the T1-weighted image with niimath's implementation of AFNI's 3dAllineate [10], using the local Pearson correlation cost [11] and a 12-parameter affine transform. Slice-timing correction (Fourier phase shift to the middle of each volume's acquisition) was applied to runs with slice-timing information; that reference time is recorded as StartTime in each output sidecar. Head-motion correction, distortion correction, coregistration and normalisation were composed into a single cubic B-spline interpolation, giving data in T1w space (at the functional resolution) and in MNI152NLin2009cAsym space. Confounds were computed as in fMRIPrep, including framewise displacement and DVARS [12] and anatomical and temporal CompCor components [13]. Temporal SNR was the mean divided by the residual standard deviation after regressing polynomial trends and the six motion parameters. No spatial smoothing or temporal filtering was applied.

Distortion correction for task-rest: the field was estimated from a pair of EPI images with opposite phase encoding using SuCor [14] and applied as a voxel-shift map with Jacobian intensity modulation, followed by a residual correction: the reference was converted to a synthetic T1-weighted image with SynthSR [15], and a diffeomorphic Greedy [9] registration of the subject's brain-extracted T1-weighted image to it (both brain-extracted with MindGrab) gave a 3D displacement field applied after coregistration.
